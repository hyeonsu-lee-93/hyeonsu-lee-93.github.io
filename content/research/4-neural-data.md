In collaboration with experimental labs, I analyze population recordings and imaging data to find how neural activity
patterns relate to behavior. Examples include the cortico-cortical pathway that coordinates sequential bimanual
movements in mice, distinct types of projection neurons that process working memory and timing in parallel, and a
classification method for spatiotemporal activity patterns in brain imaging data.
