---
title: "Paper on visual quantity comparison published in *Cell Reports*"
date: 2023-07-29
summary: Dr. Choi and I published a paper proposing the spontaneous emergence of quantity comparison ability.
---

Dr. Choi and I published a paper demonstrating the spontaneous emergence of quantity comparison ability.
Our research showed that single neurons tuned to ratios and differences in visual quantities can arise spontaneously in completely untrained neural networks, enabling the network to perform quantity comparison tasks.
I am deeply grateful for the guidance and supervision of Prof. Se-Bum Paik.

It was also introduced in several news outlets, including [KAIST news](https://news.kaist.ac.kr/news/html/news/?mode=V&mng_no=30790).
