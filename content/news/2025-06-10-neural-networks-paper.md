title: "Paper on continual learning published in *Neural Networks*"
date: 2025-06-10
summary: Suhee and I published a paper proposing a working memory-like memory model that resolves catastrophic forgetting.
---

My mentee, Suhee Cho, and I recently published a paper presenting a metaplasticity model inspired by human working memory.
In this study, based on my previous work [(Lee et al., 2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2019.09.034), we implemented distinct types of synapses from stable to flexible, and randomly intermixed them to train synaptic connections with varying degrees of flexibility.
We found that our model achieved a balanced tradeoff between memory capacity and performance without requiring additional training or structural modifications, dynamically allocating memory resources to retain both old and new information.
